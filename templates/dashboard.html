Clinical Pipeline Protocol

Automated
  • 1
    Quality & Integrity Validation

    Automated validation checks pixel depth, histogram distribution, and motion artifacts.

  • 2
    BFS Component Analysis (DSA)

    Traverses the 2D pixel grid graph using 8-connected BFS to detect contiguous anomaly clusters.

  • 3
    ROI Bounding Box Generation

    Extracts coordinates and marks detected regions of interest on the scan without black-box ML.

  • 4
    Statistical Evaluation

    Computes mathematical variance and intensity deviation against clinical thresholds.

Physician Review Required

MedicMitra acts strictly as a computer-aided triage and decision support system. Final diagnostic sign-off must always be executed by a certified physician.
