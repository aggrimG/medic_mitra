Radiometric Processing Pipeline

  • 1
    Contrast Equalization (CLAHE)

    Applies adaptive histogram optimization to enhance tissue contrast and subtle infiltrate margins.

  • 2
    High-Density Opacity Thresholding

    Isolates high-attenuation pixels exceeding transparent lung baseline thresholds.

  • 3
    Morphological Cluster Segmentation

    Connected-component grouping segments contiguous focal consolidation regions.

    2-Page Diagnostic Report Generation

    Compiles quantitative radiomics, plain-language patient explanations, and signature attestation.
